Usually Bills are in the form CCC_T_XXXX Where:
	- CCC is the congress number (ex:119)
	- T is the Bill type enum (0-3 are Senate origin and 4-7 are House origin)
	- XXXX is the Bill number (ex: HR *3923* )

When the Bill number is beyond 9999 we get ids of the form CCC_T_XXXXX, with an extra X integer at the end
	- This is just because of poor planning when designing the primary ids, fixing would
		require literally deleting and recreating all the rows in db so thats not happening
