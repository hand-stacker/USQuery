How to request deletion

To request deletion of your account and associated personal data, press the button below. This will schedule your account for deletion. A scheduled account will be permanently deleted when the site operator runs the deletion job (typically every 30 days).

What data is deleted or kept

  • Deleted: your User account and any personal information associated with the account. This includes profile data, email, devices, starred items, etc.
  • Kept: any data from other systems or backups may be retained according to our retention/backup policy.
